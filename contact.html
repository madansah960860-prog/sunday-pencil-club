Contact the Sunday Pencil Club

By the Sunday Pencil Club Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

We read every message ourselves, and nothing cheers an editor more than a note from a fellow solver. The best way to reach us is by email — there is no form to fill in and no account to make.

Write to us

Send your note to puzzles@sundaypencilclub.com. Tell us which puzzle you were enjoying and what is on your mind, and we will do our best to help. We aim to reply within two business days.

Spotted a trivia answer you believe is wrong, or a typo in a clue? Our editors would be grateful to hear at editor@sundaypencilclub.com. We would far rather be corrected than stay mistaken.

A few common questions

Is the Sunday Pencil Club really free?
Completely. Every puzzle is free to solve, there is nothing to buy, and we take no payments and run no advertising.
Do I need to sign up or make an account?
No. Just open a page and start solving. We ask for nothing and store nothing about you beyond your text-size choice, which stays on your own device.
Can I print the puzzles?
Yes — every crossword and word search has a Print button that produces a clean black-and-white copy for pencil-and-paper solving.
I think a trivia answer is wrong. What should I do?
Please tell us at editor@sundaypencilclub.com. We check our facts carefully, but we are human, and we will happily correct any genuine error.
Will there be new puzzles?
The Daily Quiz refreshes every day, and we add new crosswords and teasers over time. Reader suggestions often shape what we make next.
