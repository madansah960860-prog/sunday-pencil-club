About the Sunday Pencil Club

By the Sunday Pencil Club Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

The Sunday Pencil Club is a small, independent puzzle page made for the pure pleasure of solving. It began with a simple wish: a calm place to find a good crossword, an honest quiz, and a riddle worth repeating — without advertisements shouting over the clues, without a sign-up form between you and the fun, and without anyone trying to sell you a thing.

What we make

Everything on these pages is original and made by hand. We build each crossword square by square, choosing everyday words and writing clues that are fair rather than fiendish. We dream up the word searches and the brain teasers ourselves. And we gather our trivia from the kind of well-settled facts a curious person collects over a lifetime, checking each answer before it joins the bank.

Who it is for

We have the unhurried solver in mind, and the older solver especially. The type is large and grows larger at the touch of a button. The grids are roomy, the clues are plain, and anything you can do on the screen you can print and do with a pencil instead. Our aim is a page that welcomes you in and never makes you feel rushed, tested, or talked down to.

Free, and staying that way

Sunday Pencil Club is an independent puzzle page for the simple pleasure of it. It costs nothing to solve here, there is nothing to buy, and we take no payments of any kind. We run no advertising and ask for no account. Sunday Pencil Club is an independent educational website and is not affiliated with any newspaper, publisher, or game maker; where a word or theme happens to resemble a famous puzzle, it is coincidence and fondness, never imitation.

A small promise

We keep a short list of promises and mean every one. The puzzles will always be original and the clues always fair. The trivia will always be checked, and corrected gladly when we slip. The pages will stay free, plain, and calm, with nothing to buy and no one trying to sell you anything. And your visit will be yours alone — we gather nothing about you beyond the text size you choose, which never leaves your device. That is the whole character of the Club.

About the photographs

The handful of photographs here are openly licensed images from public collections, downloaded and kept on our own pages rather than borrowed from across the web. Where a license asks for credit, we name the photographer beneath the picture, and a full list lives in the site's image credits file.

Drop us a line

We love to hear from solvers — a puzzle you enjoyed, a clue that stumped you, a theme you would like to see, or a correction we need to make. Reader notes genuinely shape what we build next, so please do not be shy. Write any time to puzzles@sundaypencilclub.com, and see our Contact page for what to expect.
